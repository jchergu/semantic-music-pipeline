The inset re-plots the first 50 ranks. It is not decoration: the two curves are
indistinguishable past roughly rank 30, so without it the figure would show a
single visible line and the reader would have to take the difference on trust.

**What this figure must not be made to say.** The peak falls 38.8% while Gini
moves 0.0044 (about 1.1% of its pre-fix value). Both are annotated, deliberately,
because the two statistics disagree about the size of the fix and §5.5 reports
that disagreement rather than leading with the flattering number.

| "of 4,110" | `…signal_contribution.total_rows` | §2 Metric 1 |
| "100.0%" similarity-pool footnote | `…signal_contribution.pct_in_similarity_pool` | §2 Metric 1 |

The four classes are mutually exclusive and sum to 4,110 exactly (verified).

**Labels are deliberately not the artifact's key names.** `genre_only` is
rendered "Similarity + genre sibling", and `artist_only` as "Similarity + same
artist", because `pct_in_similarity_pool` is 100.0: every one of the 4,110 rows
reached the ranking function through the similarity pool, so a boost is always
an addition to a similarity score, never an alternative route into the list.
Printing the raw key names would imply four independent candidate sources,
which is the opposite of how the pipeline works.

## Style provenance

Mechanics follow the eval packs' data plots (`eval/8_1/run.py::write_figures`,
`eval/8_2/run.py::write_figures`): Agg backend, `figsize=(8, 4.5)`,
`tight_layout()`, one figure per file. Thesis-facing conventions follow the
neighbouring `make_82_diagrams.py`: the muted print-safe palette (amber
`#a8792a`, blue `#2f5c8a`, purple `#6b4a86`, green `#4a7a3c` — distinguishable
in greyscale when paired with distinct linestyles), the in-figure
`Figure 5.N — …` title, and `dpi=200, facecolor="white"` on save.

Chapter 6 references thesis-owned figures as `figures/fig-6-N-*.png` at
`{width=6.4in}` and eval-pack figures in place at `{width=6.0in}`. These three
are thesis-owned; `{width=6.0in}` suits their aspect ratio. Section 3 of the
plan owns the actual `![…]` references and captions — this session wrote none.
